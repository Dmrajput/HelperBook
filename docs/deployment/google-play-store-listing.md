## Title

HelperBook

## Short description

Staff attendance, leave, salary, and advances for a small shop.

## Full description

HelperBook helps a small business keep staff details in one place.

You can:

- Add employees and turn on a separate employee login.
- Mark daily attendance, including bulk updates.
- Record leave and choose whether approved sick leave is paid.
- Calculate salary from attendance and record salary payments.
- Track advances and repayments.
- Open reports and salary receipts.
- Use a free plan or a paid monthly plan for more active employees.

The app needs an internet connection. It does not file taxes and it does not replace an accountant.

Paid plans, if they remain in the Play build, are billed as described on the subscription screen. Prices shown in the current code are Free, Starter at ₹19 per month, Business at ₹49 per month, and Pro at ₹149 per month.
